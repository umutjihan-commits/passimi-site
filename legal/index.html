It applies to everyone who uses the app; the sections for specific countries add to it.

Location — Travel Mode

Turning on Travel Mode is entirely optional. When you do, the app uses your device location to work out which country you are in.

  • The country is determined on your device
  • Only the country code and the date are sent to our servers
  • Raw GPS coordinates are never stored or transmitted
  • The trip planner may use your last known location once, on your device, to suggest your starting city; it is not sent to us
  • You can turn it off at any time in the app or in your device settings

Notifications

Enabling notifications is optional. When you do, we store a notification identifier (push token) for your device, with its platform and the app language so that announcements reach you in the right language.

  • This identifier does not directly identify you; it is a technical address for your device
  • It is used only for document expiry reminders and important announcements relevant to you
  • Trip reminders (for example "apply for your visa by…") are scheduled on your device and are not sent through our servers
  • It is never used for advertising or marketing
  • You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings, which invalidates the identifier

Retention

  • Account, document, travel and trip data: kept for as long as your account is active. A trip plan you delete is removed from our servers at the next sync
  • When you delete your account: all of your personal data — including documents, travel records, trip plans, feedback, notification identifier and consent records — is erased immediately from our active databases and is no longer accessible
  • How we destroy data: records are permanently deleted from the database so that they cannot be restored; no paper copies exist
  • Encrypted backups: residual copies may persist in our infrastructure provider's encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which the ordinary backup cycle overwrites them permanently
  • Server logs: retained by our hosting provider for no longer than 30 days, solely for security monitoring, abuse prevention and troubleshooting
  • Notification identifier: invalidated when you turn notifications off or when device access ends
  • Username change attempts: kept for 1 day to prevent abuse

Account deletion cannot be undone. If you want to keep your records, export them from Settings before you delete.

DAY COUNTS AND COUNTERS

The Schengen 90/180 counter, residence permit day counters and residency or citizenship goal counters may produce incorrect results. Reasons include:

  • Location detection identifying the wrong country, working late, or not working at all
  • Location permission being off, battery saving, or background activity restrictions
  • Time zone differences and border crossings around midnight
  • Errors or omissions in dates you enter manually
  • Transit stops, same-day entries and exits, or partial days being counted differently from one country to another

RESIDENCY AND CITIZENSHIP GOALS

Goal tracking in the app is a personal note-keeping tool based only on the data you enter. Authorities rely on their own records when assessing residence and citizenship applications, and those records may differ from the app's figures. A counter showing that you have reached a target does not mean an application will be approved.

VISA AND TRAVEL INFORMATION

Visa and visa-free information in the app may be incomplete, out of date or simply wrong. Visa rules depend not only on your nationality but also on your age, passport type, purpose of travel, length of stay, dual nationality, previous visa refusals, vaccination and health requirements, and exceptions in bilateral agreements. The app may not reflect all of these conditions.

For example, a country may grant a visa exemption only to a particular age group or only to certain passport types; the app may not show that distinction, or may show it incorrectly.

TRIP PLANNER ESTIMATES

Flight, train, road and ferry times, distances, routes drawn on the map, airports and connections suggested, time zone and arrival-date calculations, and visa application lead times (including appointment waiting times) are rough estimates. They are not timetables or official processing times, and real waiting times at a consulate can be much longer. Check schedules with the carrier and processing times with the authority before you book or apply.
